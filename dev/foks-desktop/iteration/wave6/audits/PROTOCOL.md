# Round-two persona audits — against wave 6

The five personas from `../../wave3/AUDIT-BRIEF.md` walk the same goals again,
this time against the implemented combination in `../01-vault.html` and
`../02-first-run.html`, with the wave 1b variants and the wave 5 design as
reference. Each auditor is a separate Opus session, in character.

## Material every auditor receives

- `../SPEC.md` — what the combination is and where each piece came from
- `../01-vault.html`, `../02-first-run.html` — the thing under audit
- `../../BRIEF.md` §2 and `../../WAVE4-BRIEF.md` §1 — what is true of the wire
- Their own round-one report under `../../wave3/audits/` — so the round-two
  report can say what changed
- `../../wave5/` — the coherent design, for anything the combination does
  not cover (Trust, Attention inbox, Teams page, Settings)

## What each auditor reports

1. **Then and now**: their round-one top five frustrations, each marked
   Fixed / Partly / Not in wave 6, with the screen and state as evidence.
2. **A fresh transcript** of the goal against wave 6, first person, naming
   every button, label and sentence that helped or misled.
3. **Remaining issues**, as a table with columns: `#`, `Severity`
   (blocker / major / minor), `Where` (file and state), `What`, `Ask` (the
   change in the persona's words), `Needs protocol?` (yes / no).
4. **Anything wrong or missing about FOKS** on any screen, checked against
   BRIEF §2 and WAVE4-BRIEF §1.
5. **One sentence** on whether they would use it.

Reports are saved verbatim under this directory as
`0N-<persona>-round2.md`, and the product owner receives a single
consolidated, structured list.
